# 📈 Superstore Sales Performance & Growth Sustainability Analysis

An end-to-end Retail Business Intelligence project combining an **interactive web dashboard** with a **C-suite executive forensic report** analyzing $2.26M in revenue across 4,922 orders (2015–2018).

## 📌 Executive Problem Statement: Is Growth Sustainable?
While headline revenues grew by **+20.3%** in 2018 ($722K), forensic cohort analysis revealed structural vulnerabilities:
* **The Acquisition Cliff:** New customer intake collapsed by **-98%** (from 589 to just 11 accounts), with new accounts generating only **1%** of 2018 revenue.
* **Extraction vs. Expansion:** Growth was driven entirely by existing accounts ordering more frequently (1.6 → 2.4 orders/customer) as Average Order Value (AOV) declined from $506 to $435.
* **Concentration Risks:** 33% of sales depend on two states (CA & NY), 11% on NYC alone, and 31% on just 80 accounts (top 10%).
